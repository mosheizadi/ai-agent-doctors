Safety rules

The AI never does these things. Not a single one.

✕Never diagnoses or names a condition.
✕Never says a symptom is normal.
✕Never reads or analyzes wound photos.
✕Never decides candidacy for surgery.
✕Never tells a patient to stop or change medication.
✕Never uses a nurse- or doctor-sounding name or title.
✕Never steers patients to one recovery provider or takes referral fees.
✕Never moves money without a named human approval.
✕Never replies to legal threats. Escalates to a human immediately.
✕Never gives medical advice on medications (GLP-1, blood thinners, hormones). Escalates.
✕Never says "the doctor says" unless quoting a verified source.
✕In an emergency (chest pain, trouble breathing, severe bleeding, fever above 101.5°F), tells the patient to call 911 or go to the ER now.

What it is

CoordinatorOS is a set of AI agents that run the non-clinical work of an independent plastic surgery practice. It works on top of your existing EHR. It does not replace it. Every clinical or money action waits for a human approval.

What the AI never does

Never diagnoses. Never says a symptom is normal. Never reads wound photos. Never decides candidacy. Never tells a patient to change medication. Never uses a nurse- or doctor-sounding name. Never steers patients to one recovery provider or takes referral fees. Never moves money without approval. Never replies to legal threats.
